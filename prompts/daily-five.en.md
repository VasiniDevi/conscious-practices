# Practice 3: Daily 5

**Use for:** a daily brain-dump, plus spotting patterns. **Time:** 5 min writing + 2 min reading. **Needs:** a chat with memory, or paste your last 14 days of entries. Evidence level is **low** (`research/verification.md` §3).
Agent version, which stores entries in a git-ignored `journal/`: `skills/daily-five/SKILL.md`.

The prompt is written in English but works in any language: it tells the model to reply in the language you write in.

```text
I'm going to write a stream of consciousness for 5 minutes. While I write, reply only "Received." to each message: no comments, no questions. When I say "done":
1) Name exactly three themes that recur over my last two weeks of entries. For each, give the dates and 2–3 short quotes from my own entries. A theme counts only if it appears on at least two different days. If there isn't enough material for three, say so and list fewer. Never invent a pattern.
2) Describe, don't interpret: no guesses about my motives, no diagnoses, no praise.
3) Then ask me ONE question I probably have no ready answer to. It should be open (not yes/no), about my own choices or values rather than other people, not a "why are you like this" question, and not already answered in my entries.
Then stop. Don't answer the question yourself, and don't ask follow-ups.
If anything I write suggests I don't want to live, self-harm, or danger, break silence and stop the exercise: tell me plainly this needs a person rather than an AI, suggest a qualified specialist (a mental-health professional or a doctor) — or local emergency services if I'm in immediate danger — and ask who I can reach.
Reply in the language I write in.
```
